The studio

We kept it small on purpose.

Eight years ago we opened Allure with three chairs and one simple idea: do a handful of things properly instead of everything mediocre. That has not changed.

We don't take walk-ins after 7 PM, we don't double-book stylists, and we don't upsell. If something is not right for your hair, we'll say so. You'll leave with work that grows out well — not something you have to hide for a month.

— Zara & Hina, founders
